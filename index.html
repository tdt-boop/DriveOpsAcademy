Orientation

Google Drive is where your files live online instead of just on one computer — always backed up, reachable from your phone or any browser, and easy to hand to another person without emailing giant attachments.

This longer session covers 7 modules: finding your way around, uploading and organizing, searching like a pro, sharing without oversharing, managing your storage, keyboard speed tricks, and safety habits.

Module 1 · ~10 min

🗂️ Finding Your Way Around

  • 📁 My Drive — everything you personally own or uploaded.
  • 🤝 Shared with me — files others gave you access to. These don't count against your storage.
  • 🕐 Recent — your last-opened files, the fastest way back to something.
  • ⭐ Starred — files you've flagged for quick access.
  • 🗑️ Trash — deleted items sit here 30 days before they're gone for good — and still count against your storage until then.

Module 2 · ~10 min

📤 Uploading & Organizing

  • ⬆️ Drag files straight from your desktop into any Drive folder, or click "+ New."
  • 📱 On your phone, the Drive app can scan a paper document with your camera and save it as a clean PDF.
  • 🎨 Right-click any folder → Organize → Change color, so folders are easy to tell apart at a glance.
  • 🗂️ Shift + Z adds a file to a second folder without making a duplicate copy.
  • ⭐ Star anything you reopen often so it's always one click away.

Module 3 · ~10 min

🔍 Search Like a Pro

Google Drive's search bar understands special commands, not just plain words:

  • type:pdf / type:spreadsheet / type:presentation — jumps straight to that kind of file.
  • owner:me — shows only files you personally own, hiding everything just shared with you.
  • to:name@email.com — shows every file you've shared with that specific person.
  • before:2026-01-01 or after:2025-06-01 — filters by when a file was last modified.
  • Put a phrase in "quotation marks" for an exact match instead of loose keywords.

Module 4 · ~10 min

🔗 Sharing Without Oversharing

  • 👥 Click Share to add specific people by email, and pick their role: Viewer, Commenter, or Editor.
  • 🌐 "General access" can be Restricted (only people you named) or Anyone with the link — use the second one carefully.
  • ♻️ Reopen Share any time to see or remove who has access, even after you've sent it.

Module 5 · ~10 min

💾 Storage & Housekeeping

  • 📊 Every Google account starts with 15 GB, shared across Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos combined.
  • 🔎 See exactly what's eating your space at Settings → Storage — sorted largest file first.
  • 🗑️ Deleting a file only moves it to Trash — it still counts against your quota until you empty the Trash too.
  • 💳 Google One is the paid upgrade (100 GB, 200 GB, 2 TB) if you truly outgrow 15 GB — check real usage before buying.

Module 6 · ~10 min

⌨️ Speed Tricks & Shortcuts

  • Ctrl + / (⌘ + / on Mac) — pulls up the full shortcut list any time you forget one.
  • / — jumps your cursor straight into the search bar from anywhere.
  • n — renames the selected file, no right-click needed.
  • s — stars or unstars the selected file.
  • p — opens a quick preview of the selected file.
  • Arrow keys — move between files without ever touching the mouse.
